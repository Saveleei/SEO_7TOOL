## Goal

Update the local quote builder for the Russian standard VAT rate effective from 1 January 2026: use 22% as the default and calculate included VAT with the 22/122 formula, while retaining supported reduced/no-VAT choices and preserving historical saved quote revisions.

## Acceptance criteria

- New quote drafts default to 22% VAT.
- Server validation accepts 22%, 10%, and no VAT; the obsolete 20% option is not offered for new quotes.
- Included VAT is calculated correctly as total × 22/122.
- Previously saved quote revisions retain their snapshotted VAT rate and totals.
- Relevant tests, full test suite, lint, build, and local browser preview pass.
- No production/deployment/external-send changes.
