## Core scenario pages

Ten existing scenario landings (borfrezy, drilling machines, annular cutters, plate/pipe bevelers) have better differentiated copy, but most overlap with published subcategories. Current safe decision: `NOINDEX`/canonical to the closest category or subcategory; retain for advertising and conversion experiments.

## Criteria to change a decision to CREATE

All conditions are required:

1. Independent search intent confirmed in query/search-console data.
2. At least 6 stable matching product groups; target 12+ for category-like pages.
3. Unique H1/title/value proposition not equivalent to an existing category/subcategory.
4. Useful content and internal links based only on confirmed facts.
5. No competing URL with the same normalized intent key.
6. Manual review recorded in the decision allowlist/admin content.
