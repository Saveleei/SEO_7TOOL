# 7TOOL visual DNA

The system has seven permanent codes. Recognition should come from their combination; no page needs all seven at once.

## 01 — Signature Cut

- **Purpose:** turn the diagonal stroke of the number 7 into an ownable compositional operation.
- **Where to use:** one hero image plane, selected category cover, major campaign banner, active catalog state, packaging edge, or editorial opener.
- **Where not to use:** every card, every button, form fields, technical rows, body copy, icon backgrounds, or repeated patterns.
- **Frequency:** one prominent cut per major viewport; zero is acceptable on dense utility screens.
- **Examples:** the hero image field ends in a 24° cut; a selected category cover has one cut corner; a macro photo crosses a diagonal section boundary.

## 02 — Product Scale

- **Purpose:** make industrial equipment the hero and communicate commercial confidence.
- **Where to use:** hero, category covers, listing media zones, product gallery, application stories.
- **Where not to use:** tiny decorative thumbnails, busy collages, or product images behind text.
- **Frequency:** every commercial page; one dominant product per section.
- **Examples:** a magnetic drill occupies 68–78% of its image stage; a cutter uses horizontal macro scale; a CNC machine is shown with architectural breathing room.

## 03 — Technical Grid

- **Purpose:** express alignment, accuracy, and comparability without pseudo-industrial decoration.
- **Where to use:** page containers, product specs, category/filter alignment, technical articles, image captions.
- **Where not to use:** visible grid lines over all pages, decorative blueprint backgrounds, or dense crosshairs on ordinary text.
- **Frequency:** structural on every page; visible only in selected engineering moments.
- **Examples:** 12 desktop columns, shared baselines for product titles and prices, consistent left/right edges in tables.

## 04 — Calibrated Numbers

- **Purpose:** make SKUs, diameters, voltages, weights, prices, stock counts, and delivery dates a recognizable 7TOOL voice.
- **Where to use:** technical data, cards, filters, stock, captions, product gallery annotations.
- **Where not to use:** long copy, emotional headlines, or every label in monospace.
- **Frequency:** 2–5 numerical signatures per viewport.
- **Examples:** `Ø 35 мм`, `1 100 Вт`, `арт. STEYR-35`, `47 999 ₽`, with tabular figures and stable unit spacing.

## 05 — Controlled Image Field

- **Purpose:** make mixed manufacturer assets feel like one curated catalog.
- **Where to use:** all catalog, hero, application, and detail imagery through the standards in `PRODUCT_IMAGE_STANDARD.md`.
- **Where not to use:** arbitrary gradients, glows, fake metal textures, or shadows that compete with product geometry.
- **Frequency:** universal.
- **Examples:** neutral light field, common baseline, normalized subject bounds, low soft shadow, consistent crop class.

## 06 — Category Index

- **Purpose:** turn categories into a branded navigation system rather than generic tiles.
- **Where to use:** homepage category selection, mega-menu, catalog landing pages, editorial related-category links.
- **Where not to use:** product attributes, checkout, or small utility lists.
- **Frequency:** at major taxonomy entry points.
- **Examples:** large product silhouette + short category name + one quiet count + one minimal cut/line cue.

## 07 — Cut Motion

- **Purpose:** add a restrained motion signature that reinforces the cut.
- **Where to use:** hover reveal on a major category, dropdown entrance, active-filter underline, hero image reveal, drawer transition.
- **Where not to use:** continuous animation, page intro, parallax, price changes, form validation, or every card.
- **Frequency:** one motion signature per interaction group; 150–250 ms.
- **Examples:** a 16 px diagonal mask reveals on hover; active underline grows left-to-right then finishes with a short diagonal edge.

## Composition rules

- Commercial hierarchy: product → information → action → brand decoration.
- Image/brand pages: brand → product → message.
- At least two non-color codes must remain visible when orange and the logo are removed.
- A signature cut is an event, not a wallpaper.
- Large dark areas are reserved for hero, proof, quote, or footer transitions—not the default browsing surface.

## Iconography direction

- 24 px base grid; 20 px compact variant.
- 1.5 px stroke, square or minimally rounded terminals, consistent optical padding.
- Outline by default; filled state only for selected/confirmed status.
- Geometric, technical, and literal: search, compare, delivery, document, phone, cart, filter.
- No gears, sparks, mascots, mixed icon libraries, emoji, or decorative badges.
